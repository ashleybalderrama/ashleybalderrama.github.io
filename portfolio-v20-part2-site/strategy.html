Digital strategy and content

I plan the strategy, make the content and run the accounts. That covers social media, newsletters, branding, websites and video, for my own clients and as part of the team at Helena Soh Group. Open any client to see the work.

The strategist is also the photographer

I’ve been behind a camera for years, and it shapes how I approach every strategy. Photographing people, brands and communities has taught me what makes an image feel true to who it’s about.

So when I plan a brand’s visuals, I think like a photographer: what the story is, how it should look and feel, and how every image can carry the same message as the words beside it.
