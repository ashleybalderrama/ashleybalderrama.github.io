Creative direction, start to finish

On every shoot I’m the creative director as well as the photographer. I run the whole production, so the idea, the look and the final images all come from one person.

  1. Concept and art direction

    Every shoot starts with the story: what the images need to say, who they’re for and where they’ll run. I build the visual approach around that, balancing an editorial eye with the brand’s goals.

  2. Styling and the shoot

    I plan the styling and the look, set the lighting, in the studio or with natural light, and direct the session on the day.

  3. Curation and post-production

    I edit thousands of frames a year down to the ones that tell the story, sequence them, and finish them in Lightroom, Photoshop and Photo Mechanic.

  4. Delivery and the bigger picture

    I manage the client and the project from first call to final gallery, and often the brand work around it: websites, marketing materials and social content.
