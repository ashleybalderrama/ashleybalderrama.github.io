    About

    I’m a Los Angeles native and Latina photographer who turned a camera habit into a business in 2017. Through Moments I photograph brands, portraits, events and weddings, and under my own name I document community events and social justice movements across the city.

    I also work in digital strategy, helping nonprofits, mission-driven organizations and small businesses decide what to say online and then making it. I’m looking for a full-time role where the photography and the strategy can work together.
