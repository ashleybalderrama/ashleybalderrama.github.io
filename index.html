    About

    I’m a Los Angeles native and Latina photographer who turned a camera habit into a business in 2017. Through Moments I photograph brands, portraits, events and weddings. Under my own name I document community events and social justice movements across the city, including as a regular contributor to LAist.

    Since 2022 I’ve also worked as a digital strategy coordinator with Helena Soh Group, where I help nonprofits, public figures and mission-driven organizations figure out what to say online and then make it, schedule it and measure it. I do the same for my own clients, from a team of financial advisors to a fine art painter.

    Before that I spent four years in live theatre, working up from production intern to associate producer. It taught me how to run a production, a budget and a room, and I still use all three on every shoot.

    I’m looking for a full-time role where the photography and the strategy can work together.
